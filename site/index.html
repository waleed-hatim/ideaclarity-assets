THE METHOD

Most ideas don't fail because they're bad. They fail because nobody checked.

Idea Clarity borrows from design thinking, strategy consulting and product management: understand the person before the product, find the risk before the roadmap, and let small tests decide instead of opinions.

STAGE 1

Get clear

Put the idea on one page: who it's for, what they do today, and what would change.

STAGE 2

Find the risk

Sort every belief into known, assumed and unknown. Test first the one that's critical with no evidence.

STAGE 3

Test it

Past beats future. Ask what people did last time, not whether they'd use your app. Then ask them to pay.

STAGE 4

Decide

Keep going, adjust, or stop, based on what people actually did. Then run the next test.

THREE TRAPS WE HELP YOU AVOID

The mistakes that cost founders months

TRAP 1

"Everyone loves it"

Compliments are free. We swap "would you use this?" for "tell me about the last time this happened", and that answer is much harder to fake.

TRAP 2

Building first, asking later

An app, a website or a first batch feels like progress. A pre-sale, a paper version or ten conversations usually teach you more, in days and for almost nothing.

TRAP 3

Testing the easy part

It's tempting to test what you're sure of. We point you at the belief that would sink the idea if it's wrong, and test that first.
